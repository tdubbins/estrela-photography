About

hello, I'm Esther

I believe the most beautiful images are the ones that feel like a quiet memory — soft light, genuine laughter, a hand held just a bit tighter.

For years I've been drawn to the moments between moments: the pause before a kiss, the tumble of a child, the calm weight of a pregnant belly in morning light. That's what I photograph.

My work is unhurried and warm. I won't pose you stiffly or promise a polished magazine cover. What I will do is notice — and keep — the truest version of who you are, right now.

— Esther
