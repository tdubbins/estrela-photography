The Observer

To me, photography is art. It is the closest thing to time travel we have; a gentle way to frame your essence in a fast-paced world.

I am the observer. Ever since I was a child, I subconsciously knew how valuable memories are. Now, out here in the golden light of the Algarve, I create safe, quiet spaces where you can completely forget about the lens.

When people tell me my photographs are still on their walls years later, that is when my soul smiles.

Esther
