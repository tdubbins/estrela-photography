Privacy Notice

how your data is handled

Who is Responsible

The person responsible for the processing of your personal data (the "data controller") is Esther Mathilde Bergstra, a private individual working under the name Estrela Photography. You can reach me at estrelaphotography@outlook.com. Full contact details are given in the Legal Notice.

What We Collect

We only collect the personal data you choose to share with us, namely:

  • Your name and contact details (email, phone) when you enquire about or book a session.
  • The content of the messages you send us by email, WhatsApp, or social media.
  • Photographs taken during your session, once you have booked with us.

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no contact form, no analytics, no advertising, and sets no tracking cookies.

Why We Use It & Legal Basis

We process your data only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and arrange sessions — on the basis of taking steps at your request prior to a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To carry out an occasional session you have arranged and issue the individual invoice for it — to perform our agreement and meet Portuguese tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR).
  • To display your photographs in our portfolio or on social media — only with your separate, freely given consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time.

How Long We Keep It

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to answer you and for a reasonable period afterwards. Data relating to a booking is kept for the duration of our working relationship and for as long as required by Portuguese tax and accounting law thereafter. Photographs are retained in line with the terms agreed with you.

Who We Share It With

We do not sell your data. It may be handled by service providers who help us operate — such as our website host (GitHub) and email provider (Microsoft Outlook) — who process it only on our behalf. Where a provider is located outside the EU, appropriate safeguards apply under the GDPR.

Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Have inaccurate data corrected or incomplete data completed.
  • Request erasure of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to how we process your data.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email estrelaphotography@outlook.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD (cnpd.pt).

This privacy notice is provided in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Portuguese Law n.º 58/2019. Last updated: July 2026.
